# Naming and trademark research

Researched 13 Oct 2026. This is a self-run pre-screen, **not legal advice**. Fees were checked
against official office pages. Name checks used:
- **Trademarks:** the USPTO trademark search, plus TMview for the EU-wide, UK, WIPO and Bulgarian
  registers.
- **Apps:** the App Store (US, UK and DE storefronts) and Google Play.
- **Domains:** the domain registries' own lookup services (RDAP).
- **Social handles:** Bluesky, TikTok and YouTube.
- **Companies:** UK Companies House and OpenCorporates.

**Recommendation:** Readfellow. File it as an EU trademark in classes 9, 38, 42 and 45 for
€1,200, then decide on the US and the UK within 6 months. One check is still open: the EU
national registers (see below).

## Classes

The edition in force is the 13th, NCL 13-2026. The official class notes put "online forums" and
"chat rooms" in class 38 whatever their subject. Discord, Reddit and Wattpad all claim class 38.

| Tier | Class | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | 9 | The downloadable app |
| Essential | 45 | Online social networking |
| Recommended | 42 | The web platform and a future subscription |
| Recommended | 38 | The chapter discussions (forums, chat) |
| Optional | 41 | Book information and book clubs. Add it later if the app publishes its own content. |

Draft wording follows. **Verify each term in TMclass (tmclass.tmdn.org) before filing.**

- **9:** Downloadable software applications; Application software
- **38:** Providing online forums; Providing internet chatrooms
- **42:** Software as a service [SaaS]; Platform as a service [PaaS]
- **45:** Online social networking services

Do not use any wording about publishing, selling or providing fan fiction or literary works. Never
name AO3 or Open Library in the specification.

## Costs (4 classes: 9, 38, 42, 45)

| Route | Official fees | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **EU trademark** (covers Bulgaria) | **€1,200** (€850 + €50 + €150 + €150) | You can file yourself; an applicant living in the EU needs no representative. Third parties have 3 months after publication to oppose. |
| US, direct | $1,400 ($350 per class) | A US-licensed attorney is mandatory for foreign-domiciled applicants (37 CFR 2.11). An intent-to-use filing adds $150 per class later, to prove use. Filing based on the EU mark avoids that. The current US wait is 4.3 months to first action and 10.4 months to registration. |
| UK, direct | £385 (£205 + 3 × £60) | You need a UK address for service. Registration takes about 3–4 months. |
| Madrid, based on the EU mark, covering US + UK | CHF 2,925 (653 basic + 1,840 US + 432 UK), plus the €1,200 EU mark | One filing and one renewal. If the EU mark falls within 5 years, the US and UK protection falls with it. |
| Bulgarian national mark | €306.76 | Not needed if you file an EU trademark. |

The EU SME Fund trade mark voucher (75% refund, up to €700) has run out for 2026. It is open only
to registered businesses and the self-employed, not private individuals.

**Not confirmed from official text:**
- Whether an EU trademark filing can serve as the priority basis for US and UK filings. Art. 32
  EUTMR treats it as equivalent to a national filing, so it should.
- The typical EU registration time.
- The effect of EUIPO fast track.

## Plan (no lawyer)

1. Run the manual TMview check for Readfellow.
2. Secure the brand. Try to buy readfellow.com and register readfellow.app. Claim the Bluesky,
   TikTok and YouTube handles.
3. Decide whether to file as an individual or a company. A company may qualify for the 2027
   voucher and avoids transferring the mark later.
4. File the EU trademark on euipo.europa.eu, using TMclass terms only. Do this **before** launch
   or any public announcement.
5. Within 6 months, decide on the US and the UK. Direct filing has the lowest official fees but
   needs a US attorney and a UK address. Madrid is simpler but costs more.
6. Watch the 3-month opposition window after publication.
